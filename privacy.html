Privacy Policy — homelab-backup

Last updated: 10 September 2026

homelab-backup is a personal, single-user application. Its author uses it to back up files from a private home server to the author's own Google Drive.

It reads and writes only its own backup archives in that Drive. It collects no personal data, uses no analytics or tracking, and shares nothing with third parties. There are no other users.

Archives are deleted automatically under a retention policy set by the author.

Contact: via the developer contact address registered for this application in the Google Cloud Console.
